Paw how should the layout be for this page and its sub pages
I'd structure The Cat Club (or whichever name you settle on) like a small editorial universe inside Catopia, rather than a normal website section with a bunch of article links.

The key is that the landing page should inspire exploration, while each subpage should have its own layout depending on what the content is. A breed encyclopedia shouldn't look like a product guide, and a cat history timeline shouldn't look like a quiz.

1. Overall architecture
I'd structure it like this:

THE CAT CLUB
The main discovery hub

Breeds, wild felines, anatomy
Cat Curiosities

Behaviour, science, facts
Cat Through Time

History, culture, mythology
The Cat Toolkit

Individual content pages

Articles · Guides · Comparisons · Timelines · Interactive experiences
I would keep these four major collections as the primary navigation inside this section. The homepage itself should have more discovery and editorial content, while the four collections should be accessible without making the user scroll all the way down.

7. What I would actually implement first
I'd build it in this order:

The Cat Club landing page — establish the visual identity and layout.

Meet the Cats — visual breed/species explorer and individual profiles.

Cat Curiosities — question-led article library.

The Cat Toolkit — practical care and equipment guides.

Cat Through Time — history and cultural timeline.

Unified search — across all four collections.

My Library — bookmarks and collections.

Daily Discovery — daily fact or question.

Learning Trails — curated sequences.

Interactive experiences — quizzes, breed comparisons, myth-vs-fact.

My strongest design recommendation: Make The Cat Club a visual editorial world, not an article database. The landing page should make people curious enough to click, and every subpage should be designed around the type of information being explored.

If you're building this with Codex, I'd implement the landing page and the four collection structures first, but use reusable content templates so you don't end up with four disconnected mini-websites.
