Catopia cat: animation review

Frames are drawn at 1 pixel per sprite pixel and enlarged without smoothing. Pick a coat, a clip and a direction; step through frame by frame, or turn on the moving ground for walking and running to check that planted feet do not slide.

The moving ground scrolls by exactly one body step per frame, the speed the game moves the cat at that gait. A planted paw should stay on the same cobble. In the game the step cycle is driven by distance travelled, so the feet match the ground at any speed.
