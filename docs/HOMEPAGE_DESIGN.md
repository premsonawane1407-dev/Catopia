# Homepage direction

## Product story

Catopia is a thoughtful home for the cats that make campus feel like home and the people who notice them. The homepage introduces that motive, describes the idea, and makes clear that Catopia is still taking shape.

## Visual direction

The page follows the supplied editorial reference: warm paper, forest green, sage, terracotta and butter accents, bold Fraunces headlines, restrained motion, and layered portrait framing that gives the art a little depth. Its tone is personal and indie rather than a generic software landing page.

## Sections

1. **Characterful hero:** Catopia identity, a concise purpose statement, a playful cat portrait study, and links into the page.
2. **Reason:** Why campus cats deserve care and a place in the stories people remember.
3. **Interactive three-part guide:** Conceptual panels about familiar places, the cats people recognize, and small stories. The tabs are an explainer, not a live directory, profile system, map, or social feed.
4. **Animal-care promise:** Encourages noticing without disturbing cats and protecting sensitive location details.
5. **Not-open-yet close:** Says Catopia is still taking shape and does not present planned features as available.

## Image note

Images sourced from `references/cat/` are presented as portrait studies, not real Catopia profiles. Their original provenance and license are not included in the repository. Replace them with approved photographs or cleared imagery before public release. Optimized WebP copies live under `public/images/cats/`.

## Current limits

The interactive feature guide switches between three explanatory panels. There is no 3D world, map, cat community, profile system, feed, or user contribution flow on the homepage. Related assets and prototype scene code can remain staged for a later phase, but they are not rendered or described as current features.
